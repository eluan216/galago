# Galago design direction (locked)

**Product truth:** This page exists to get a Nigerian digital reseller to open a wallet and fund it. It is not a brand story deck or a generic SaaS tour.

**Audience:** Resellers and operators in Nigeria who buy engagement, VTU, numbers, and cards — often late at night, on mobile, paying in Naira or USDT.

**Direction:** Nocturnal restraint — near-black, one gold gesture, bushbaby mark, type does the hierarchy.

**Signature:** The bushbaby mark (large eyes, gold iris) + the phrase “hold until delivered.”

**Layout strategy:** Monument hero (mark + one line + one CTA). Product truth below as uneven blocks, not three equal feature cards. No logo strip. No fake testimonials.

**Tokens:** night `#0A0C10`, surface `#12161C`, moon `#F2F0EA`, mist `#8B93A7`, gold `#E8B923` only on primary action and key money moments.
